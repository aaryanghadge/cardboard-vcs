# Two-minute demo script

Record the screen with the app open on **History** (use **Reset demo** first). Speak slowly. The point to land: *editors only decide what is genuinely theirs to decide.*

## 0:00 The problem (15 seconds)

"Code has git. Video has `final_v3_actual.mp4`. This versions the edit, not the video, so a team can work on the same project in parallel and combine it back."

## 0:15 History (20 seconds)

- Point at the graph: three Alt cuts, five save points.
- Click **Shorter intro, product shot**. Read the change list: out point trimmed, a clip added.
- "Every save point is content-addressed and never edited, like a git commit."

## 0:35 A clean combine (20 seconds)

- Open **Combine**, bring in **color-pass**.
- "Dev colour-graded two clips while Priya added a lower third on another. Different clips, so no choices."
- Click **Combine into main**. Back in History, the new save point has two parents.

## 0:55 One real choice (35 seconds)

- Combine again, bring in **maya-intro**.
- Show the two lists: what Maya changed, what main changed meanwhile.
- "Everything merged on its own. Maya's intro trim and Priya's trim apply together, because they touch different ends of the clip. The only thing left is the interview's out point: 50 versus 60."
- Show the two bars. Click **Use maya-intro's**, then **Combine into main**.

## 1:30 Merge never blocks (15 seconds)

- Edit on a new Alt cut and on main so both change the same clip, then combine and click **Combine, leave 1 open**.
- "The combine succeeded and the cut plays. The choice waits in History as an open badge."

## 1:45 Close (15 seconds)

"The engine is 42 tests, the app has 15 flow tests. Next: preview the seconds around a choice, and import real Premiere and Resolve timelines through OpenTimelineIO."
